## Manufacturing processes

Enclosure: injection moulding of UV-resistant ABS or polycarbonate. Includes vibration-dampening mounts and quick-release bike attachment   ( #IP should we make the mounting part proprietary so it could also be an IP? #decide ).

Electronics: surface mount technology on multi layer PCBs with conformal coating ( #PD waterproof and dust proof rating target #decide ).

Sensor integration: LiDAR module mounted and calibrated; IMU aligned  GPS ( #PD #MR IMU inclusion. do we even need this? #decide ).

Power: LiPo battery pack with thermal management and USB-C charging. Initial low-volume assembly in EU facility; later scale to Asia  ( #DP #MR im thinking of a phased manufacturing location strategy #propose ).

## Supply chain

LiDAR modules from specialist suppliers ( #DP this would have long lead times impact on schedule #inform ).

Electronics from qualified EU vendors to cut risk and tariffs. Mechanical parts from local suppliers.

ERP for inventory and seasonal cycling-peak forecasting. Sustainability: recycled plastics preferred, audited suppliers  ( #FI conflict minerals compliance approach #decide ).
