## Meeting Outcome 

- Reviewed Coursework A questions / requirements to guide brainstorming of ideas 
- Assigned preliminary roles 
- Pitched and discussed multiple product concepts 

## Three Product Ideas: 

- A product focused on reducing the environmental impact of AI systems by improving energy efficiency and computational sustainability. 
- An attachable PPS (programmable power supply) chip for USB-C chargers that would allow any device to be compatible with PPS technology and variable voltages, appealing to a wider market of consumer devices, to popularize faster and more efficient charging.
- A portable diesel-powered generator system capable of charging electric vehicles in emergency situations, configurable for different EV models and brands. 

## Agreed Actions 

- Conduct preliminary market research on all three concepts 
- Investigate existing patents and competing solutions 
- Evaluate (briefly) feasibility, scalability, and differentiation 
- Select one concept to progress into development for the next phase
