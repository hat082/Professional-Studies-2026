## Meeting Outcome 
 
Agreed on what the product is, problems it solves, business plan, business targets, uniqueness of the product. 

1. The Product & Capabilities: An IoT sensor package (LiDAR, radar, cameras) that warns cyclists of hazards in real-time while generating road condition data for government maintenance. 

2. Problem Statement: Addresses the lack of commercial bicycle safety standards and high cyclist fatality rates (e.g., 36% in the Netherlands) caused by collisions and poor infrastructure. 

3. Unique Selling Point (USP): The only commercial solution combining pothole detection and traffic awareness, filling a gap left by academic prototypes and automotive-focused tech. 

4. Target Market & Business Model: A business model combining B2C subscriptions for safety-conscious cyclists with B2B/B2G data services selling road reports to insurers and governments. 

5. Future Expansion Ideas: Plans to integrate augmented reality (AR) helmets for heads-up alerts and expand the technology into the motorcycle market.
